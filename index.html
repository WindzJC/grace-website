Three things this book wants you to know

  1. 1

    Nothing is impossible

    Not in the sight of God. That is the promise the book was written to leave with whoever reads it.

  2. 2

    You were born with a purpose

    Grace starts from the conviction that every person on earth is born with a reason to be here.

  3. 3

    The potential is already yours

    Faith comes with a challenge: you have what it takes to make it in life, if you truly want to.

Nothing, the book says, is more

Awesome, Special and Unique

than you.

About the author

Grace J. Mbong

Grace was born in Pennsylvania and attended Chattahoochee Technical College. Since 2009 she has published three short books of faith-filled encouragement.

What sets them apart is who they are written to. Every title speaks straight to the reader, as “you”: you have what it takes; God has not forgotten you; what is in your hands can give you millions.
