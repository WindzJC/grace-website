About the author

Words written for you.

Grace J. Mbong

Grace was born in Pennsylvania and attended Chattahoochee Technical College. Her published books speak to faith, purpose, and the gifts each person carries.

Across three books, Grace addresses her readers directly. Her message is plainspoken and rooted in faith: you have purpose, you are not forgotten, and what you carry has value.
