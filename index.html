For the days when you are unsure of what you can become.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORDS

Meet Grace
J. Mbong.

Grace was born in Pennsylvania and attended Chattahoochee Technical College, according to her publisher's author biography. Her published books speak to faith, human potential, and purpose.

There is a personal quality shared by the three titles: each speaks to the reader directly. Not to a crowd, but to you.
