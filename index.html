THE PERSON BEHIND THESE WORDS

Grace J.
Mbong.

An author of short works on faith, purpose, and the potential within every person.

Grace was born in Pennsylvania and attended Chattahoochee Technical College, according to her publisher biography. Her three books are brought together here so readers can move easily from one message to the next.

Her writing repeatedly returns to a simple idea: the reader matters. Each of these titles is addressed directly to you.
